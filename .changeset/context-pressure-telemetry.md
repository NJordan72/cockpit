Context-window pressure bar and session/task telemetry: the Sessions panel and bottom toolbar now show a compact context-window pressure bar beside token counts (amber at 70%, red at 85% against a 200k default context limit) with one-click **Compact** (`/compact`) and **Fresh** pane actions when pressure is elevated or from the right-click menu. Cockpit also tracks cumulative active working time and tool-call counts per session and rolls them up alongside token counts on Tasks and Runs rows.
