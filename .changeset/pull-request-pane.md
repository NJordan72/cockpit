Click a pull request in the Pull requests panel to open it in a pane of its own instead of on GitHub. The pane shows the review threads with the lines they're about, the comments and reviews, and the checks. Reply to a thread and resolve it, comment, approve or request changes, mark a draft ready, merge, or close, all through your own `gh` login, and Cockpit asks before merging or closing. **→ Agent** sends a thread, a comment, a review, or a failing check (with the end of its log) to an agent: the one working in the pull request's task, a new task on its branch, or any other agent pane. Tick several to send them together.
