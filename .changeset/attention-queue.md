Attention queue and quick triage: press Cmd+Shift+U (`nextAttention`) or click the **N waiting** badge in the toolbar to jump to the oldest pane waiting for input across windows and background runs, then done panes, cycling on repeated presses. Waiting panes in the Sessions panel also show inline Approve (Enter), Deny (Esc), Reply, and Focus actions (plus a context menu) so simple prompts can be answered without switching tabs.
