Unified command palette: press `Cmd+P` (or click **⌕ Palette** in the toolbar) to fuzzy-search and jump to any open session, task, run, quick command, slash command, layout, theme, or core app action. Searches everything by default, with optional `>` (commands/actions) and `@` (sessions) prefixes or `Tab` scope cycling.
